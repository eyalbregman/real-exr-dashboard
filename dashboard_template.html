About These Price Indices
Israel CPI

Israel's official Consumer Price Index, published monthly by the Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS index 120010, "Consumer Price Index - General"). It tracks the average change in prices paid by households for a fixed basket of goods and services, and is the index used for indexation of contracts, rent, and alimony. This is the original, not seasonally adjusted series -- CBS publishes a separate seasonally-adjusted version for analytical use.

US CPIAUCSL

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics' headline Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, tracking average price changes for a fixed basket of consumer goods and services. This is the seasonally adjusted version, with predictable seasonal effects (weather, holidays, school year, etc.) removed so month-to-month changes are easier to read.

US CPIAUCNS

The same Consumer Price Index as CPIAUCSL, but not seasonally adjusted -- the raw published figures before removing seasonal effects.

US PCEPI

The Personal Consumption Expenditures Price Index from the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis. Unlike CPI's fixed basket, it reflects the actual, changing mix of goods and services people buy -- this is the inflation gauge the Federal Reserve targets for its 2% goal. Seasonally adjusted.

US PCEPILFE

"Core" PCE -- the same index as PCEPI but excluding food and energy prices, which tend to swing sharply from month to month for reasons unrelated to underlying inflation. Also from the BEA and seasonally adjusted; this is the measure the Fed watches most closely to judge the underlying inflation trend.
